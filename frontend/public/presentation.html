The Problem vs Solution
02 / 09

Why Cater-POS?

Eliminating restaurant bottlenecks through digital table self-service & automated dispatch.

❌

Traditional Restaurant Challenges

• Waiters manually writing down orders causing delays & human errors.

• Kitchen line misplacing paper tickets during peak rush hours.

• Customers waiting 15+ minutes just to get a waiter for water or the bill.

• High staff overhead and disjointed cash register drawer reconciliation.

✅

The Cater-POS Solution

• Contactless QR: Guests order instantly from table with modifiers.

• Real-time KDS: Kitchen receives cooking tickets with chime alerts.

• Instant Service Bell: 1-click calls for Ice, Cutlery, Cleaning, or Bill.

• Live Shift Sales Tracking: Automated operator shift attribution & drawer reports.

Live Demo Script
08 / 09

6-Step End-to-End Demonstration

Follow this sequence to present a complete dining lifecycle to stakeholders.

1
Guest Places Order: Scan Table QR, customize food with modifiers, submit order (Status: Pending).
2
Cashier Approves: Cashier clicks [Approve Order ✅] ➔ System logs `accepted_by` and dispatches to kitchen.
3
Kitchen Cooks: Chef hears audio chime & sets status to Cooking 🔥 (Item locks on POS).
4
Guest Calls Service: Guest taps "Request Ice 🧊" ➔ Live banner appears on Cashier screen.
5
Checkout & Settle: Cashier clicks [Pay Bill 💳] (Cash / KHQR / Card) ➔ Sales credited to Cashier Shift.
6
Close Session: Cashier clicks [Close Session] ➔ Table resets to Free & QR token expires.
