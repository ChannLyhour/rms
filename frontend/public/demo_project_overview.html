🌟 1. Project Overview & Architecture

Cater-POS is a modern, reactive restaurant management platform designed to automate table dining sessions, streamline kitchen line preparation, and give cashiers instant control over running tabs and payment settlements.

📱

Guest QR Ordering

Zero-app guest ordering via dynamic Table QR codes with custom modifiers and real-time cooking status.

💻

Cashier Live Station

Shift sales tracking, service bell assistance, one-click order approval, and running tab settlement.

👨‍🍳

Kitchen KDS Line

Chronological preparation tickets with sound alerts, live cooking timers, and instant cook line updates.

📊

Admin Analytics

Staff shift sales ranking by role, table occupancy metrics, and ingredient stock level management.

🔄 2. End-to-End Live Demonstration Workflow

Step Role & Screen Action & System Event Real-time Outcome
01 Customer QR Guest scans Table QR, selects meals & modifiers, clicks Place Order. Order created in Pending state.
02 Cashier POS Receives real-time banner alert & clicks Approve Order ✅. Auto-records accepted_by & accepted_role.
03 Kitchen KDS Kitchen receives ticket chime & chef clicks Start Cooking 🔥. Items locked from editing in POS.
04 Service Bell Guest clicks Request Water / Ice 🧊 on mobile. Cashier receives table assistance card.
05 Settlement Cashier clicks Pay Bill 💳 (Cash Drawer / KHQR / Card). Sales attributed to Cashier's shift.
06 Close Session Cashier clicks Close Session. Table resets to Available & QR token expires.
